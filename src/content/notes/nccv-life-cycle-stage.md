<h2 data-lang-zh="生命史视角" data-lang-en="A Life-history Perspective">生命史视角</h2>

<p data-lang-zh="东北冷涡不只是一个固定时刻的低涡中心。若要讨论云和降水过程，需要先把事件放回形成、发展、成熟和消散的时间轴中，再比较不同阶段的动力背景和云微物理响应。" data-lang-en="A Northeast China Cold Vortex is not only a low-vortex center at a single moment. To discuss cloud and precipitation processes, the event should first be placed along a timeline of formation, development, maturity, and decay, and then compared across different dynamical backgrounds and cloud microphysical responses.">东北冷涡不只是一个固定时刻的低涡中心。若要讨论云和降水过程，需要先把事件放回形成、发展、成熟和消散的时间轴中，再比较不同阶段的动力背景和云微物理响应。</p>

<h2 data-lang-zh="可用指标" data-lang-en="Possible Metrics">可用指标</h2>

<ul>
  <li data-lang-zh="冷涡中心位置与移动路径" data-lang-en="Vortex-center position and movement track">冷涡中心位置与移动路径</li>
  <li data-lang-zh="位势高度或涡度强度变化" data-lang-en="Changes in geopotential height or vorticity intensity">位势高度或涡度强度变化</li>
  <li data-lang-zh="云量、云相态和云水路径的阶段差异" data-lang-en="Stage-dependent differences in cloud fraction, cloud phase, and cloud water path">云量、云相态和云水路径的阶段差异</li>
  <li data-lang-zh="降水区域与冷涡结构之间的相对位置" data-lang-en="Relative position between precipitation areas and the cold-vortex structure">降水区域与冷涡结构之间的相对位置</li>
</ul>

<p data-lang-zh="阶段划分不应只依赖单一阈值。更稳妥的做法是把客观识别指标和天气图解释结合起来，并记录边界样本的处理方式。" data-lang-en="Stage classification should not rely on a single threshold. A more robust approach is to combine objective identification metrics with synoptic interpretation and to document how boundary cases are handled.">阶段划分不应只依赖单一阈值。更稳妥的做法是把客观识别指标和天气图解释结合起来，并记录边界样本的处理方式。</p>
